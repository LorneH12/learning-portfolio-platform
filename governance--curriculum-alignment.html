People Through Change: An ADKAR® Introduction

Source: business/adkar-change-foundations.json

SHA-256: 8d713c5e25d608c1d6d798f135469f97827851f4789e6281852e9399b675cdb0

Audience: New managers, team leads and project contributors supporting a workplace process change.

Performance need: Teams sometimes prescribe more training for every adoption problem, overlooking unclear purpose, concerns, practical barriers or a lack of follow-through.

Quiz criterion: 6/7 correct; configured passPercent 80%; actual first attainable passing score 85.7%.

Completion: Visit all lessons and score at least 80% on the seven-item assessment (6 correct required). Untimed; retries permitted.

  • a-o1 (Understand): Distinguish the five ADKAR elements in brief workplace examples.

    • Instruction/practice: a-l1, a-l2, a-l3, a-l4; assessment: a-q1, a-q3, a-q5

  • a-o2 (Analyze): Analyze a fictional employee conversation and identify the earliest likely unmet element using stated evidence.

    • Instruction/practice: a-l2, a-l3, a-l5; assessment: a-q1, a-q2, a-q4, a-q7

  • a-o3 (Apply): Select a support action matched to the observed barrier and explain why an alternative is less useful.

    • Instruction/practice: a-l2, a-l3, a-l4, a-l5; assessment: a-q2, a-q3, a-q4, a-q5, a-q7

  • a-o4 (Create): Draft a small follow-through plan specifying a behavior, support action, observation and review date.

    • Instruction/practice: a-l4, a-l5; assessment: a-q6

Practice sequence: A new tool is not yet a new habit → Explore purpose and concerns → Separate knowing from doing → Support the behavior after launch → Coach the next step

Performance task: The create-level a-o4 objective is practiced in the lesson-five written plan. Its multiple-choice assessment items test recognition only. A facilitator can score the plan with one point each for explicit behavior, matched support, observable evidence and review date; 3/4 with behavior present is the suggested practice criterion. No automated claim that the learner has mastered creation.

Evidence limitation: Selected-response pass does not establish independent workplace competence.
