Allow 4 minutes. Relevance is more than adding a workplace photograph. In this sample, it means practicing the decision that creates a usable handoff. Motivation may include doing useful work, confidence, recognition, or simply meeting an employment requirement. Do not assume everyone arrives enthusiastic or motivated in the same way.

Worked example: replace a long department history with a short opening: “By the end, you will choose an owner and next action for a handoff so another agent can continue the case.” Let learners select a password-reset or order-status case. Both use the same required fields and performance criterion. Offer a text example, optional demonstration, and a readily available checklist. These options support access and task needs; they do not label people as fixed visual, auditory, or kinesthetic learners.

Choice has boundaries. A learner may choose a relevant practice case, but cannot skip a required safety or privacy control. An experienced user may need less explanation, while a novice may need the complete walkthrough. Check performance rather than relying only on confidence ratings.

Mini-design task: choose a workplace example for a novice and one for an experienced colleague. Keep the assessed requirement identical. Write one sentence explaining why each example is relevant.
