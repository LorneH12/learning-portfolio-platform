Allow 4 minutes. A review can be practical and brief. Start with the work requirement. Read each objective, find its practice, and identify the evidence that would show success. Flag a gap when the assessment cannot support the claim. Use Bloom’s language to explain the mismatch, not to rank people or decorate slides.

Worked example: a manager requests a 20-minute change module for ticket triage. Requirement: use the new rule card correctly. Draft A has six definition screens and a terminology quiz. Draft B has a brief rule explanation, a worked example, two supported cases, a new-case check, and a job aid. Choose B for this requirement. Keep definitions only where they enable the decision. A rollout communication about why the process is changing may still be needed, but communication and demonstrated skill answer different questions.

Review task, 60 seconds: sketch one objective, one practice task, and one assessment task for something you teach. Underline the same required action in all three. Note one tool or reference available during work that should also be available during practice.

Finish by stating the limit of your evidence. Passing a short sample check supports a narrow conclusion about these decisions. It is not a professional credential or proof of full workplace proficiency.
