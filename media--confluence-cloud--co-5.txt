Update every affected reference: Before you start, decision step, worked example, and stop-condition section. Re-run the 6-minute, 12-minute, 15-minute, and Failed-status cases. Record a concise change note so readers understand the new behavior.

Pages support version history, which helps investigate what changed. History is evidence of edits, not a substitute for checking that the current procedure is correct. If an earlier version seems preferable, compare its content and seek the appropriate review rather than blindly restoring stale instructions.

When the procedure becomes obsolete, follow the space’s approved archive and replacement-link process. Keep the canonical destination clear. The end goal is successful work: a new analyst can find the runbook, follow its decisions, and stop safely without needing the author to fill in missing steps.
