Decision scenario: Northstar’s dashboard reads “EBITDA rose 20%; the business is financially stronger.” You discover that the current number includes a newly excluded recurring expense, while last quarter did not. The cash forecast also shows a large supplier payment next week.

Step 1: ask for a like-for-like bridge, period and definition. Step 2: identify extra adjustments separately. Step 3: compare the relevant cash information before making a liquidity claim. Step 4: explain what you know, what you do not know and the next evidence needed.

Original model response: “The published figures use different adjustments, so the 20% change is not yet a like-for-like comparison. Please show both periods on the same basis and explain the added exclusion. We also need the cash forecast before concluding that next week’s supplier payment is funded.”

For a real reporting decision, involve the finance team. SEC guidance addresses non-GAAP labeling, potentially misleading adjustments and prominence of comparable GAAP information in covered disclosures. This sample is not a disclosure-compliance checklist.
