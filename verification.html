Progress rules

Read and Watch are interchangeable presentations of one lesson. Each lesson retains an independent Read panel and video bookmark. Video starts paused and never auto-completes a lesson. Learners explicitly mark lessons reviewed; watching every second is not required. All lessons and the stated assessment pass threshold are required for practice completion. Written tasks remain self-review practice and are not automatically scored.

Course progress and written notes stay in this browser. Clearing browser storage removes them. A new browser or device does not share them. The local development service uses synthetic learner records and must not be exposed as a production service.
